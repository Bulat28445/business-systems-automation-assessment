# Interview questions

Every candidate, for every open role, records a video answer to each of these six questions.

Open roles: Backend Engineer, Sales Executive, Customer Success Associate, Marketing Coordinator, Finance Analyst.

Each answer is scored from 1 to 4 against the guide under its question.

The pass mark is 3: an answer scored 3 or 4 meets the bar. Every answer is scored by two people. A candidate advances when the average of both scorers' marks across the six answers is 3 or more.

## Q1. Tell us about a piece of work you are proud of. What was your part in it?

- **1:** Cannot name a piece of work, or describes the team's work with no personal part.
- **2:** Names a piece of work but the personal contribution stays vague.
- **3:** Clear personal contribution and a result someone could check.
- **4:** Clear contribution, a measured result, and what they would do differently.

## Q2. Describe a time you had to meet a tight deadline. What did you do?

- **1:** No real example, or the deadline was simply missed with no reflection.
- **2:** An example, but the actions are generic, such as working late.
- **3:** Specific actions taken, including what was cut or delegated, and the outcome.
- **4:** Specific actions, a trade-off made on purpose, the outcome, and how they prevent a repeat.

## Q3. Tell us about a disagreement with a colleague. How did you resolve it?

- **1:** Blames the colleague, or says they never disagree with anyone.
- **2:** Describes the disagreement but not how it ended.
- **3:** Explains both views, how they listened, and how it was resolved.
- **4:** Explains both views, changed their own mind where fair, and the working relationship after.

## Q4. Tell us about a mistake you made at work. What did you learn?

- **1:** No real mistake, or the mistake was someone else's.
- **2:** A real mistake, but the lesson is generic.
- **3:** Owns the mistake, how it was found, and a specific lesson.
- **4:** Owns it, how the damage was contained, and a concrete change to how they work now.

## Q5. How do you decide what to work on first when everything feels urgent?

- **1:** No method, or whoever shouts loudest.
- **2:** A general method with no example.
- **3:** A clear way of ranking work, with a real example.
- **4:** A clear way of ranking by impact, a real example, and how they tell others what waits.

## Q6. What have you learned recently, and how did you learn it?

- **1:** Nothing specific.
- **2:** Names a topic but not how it was learned or used.
- **3:** A specific skill, how it was learned, and where it was used.
- **4:** A specific skill, a deliberate way of learning it, and a measured effect on their work.
